# App flow document

Build: quick-roi-client-copy-handoff-20261007-v5. Baselines: ../quick-roi-bottleneck-handoff-20261007-v3/03-app-flow.md and ../quick-roi-client-copy-20261007-v4/03-app-flow.md.

## Client sequence
Choose a bottleneck; enter three process values; select Calculate estimate; inspect potential annual value and assumptions; optionally select Build your business case. The external full assessment begins with contact details/consent before its comprehensive assessment. No contact details are requested or transferred from the quick tool.

## Changed and verified cues
Initial right panel now shows an empty value plus Enter your process data, then calculate your estimate. The large explanatory card is gone. Form status: Example values when untouched; Includes example values after some fields change; Your process data when all three change. A blank field focuses and displays its numeric error; a stale result disappears. Invalid scenario shows Check the highlighted inputs or reduction range; Reset reduction range restores valid output.

## Handoff and recovery
Live result and fixed HTTPS/new-tab anchor attributes were checked. Native keyboard activation opened the full assessment's blank contact intake. Contact entry/submission was not performed. The quick state remains in memory, lane changes retain entries, and reload resets them. No network retry or backend write is introduced.
