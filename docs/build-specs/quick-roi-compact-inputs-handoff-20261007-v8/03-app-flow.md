Blank submission gives specific validation and focuses first invalid input; suggestions never become values. Clear controls empty and focus one field, retain others and hide the incomplete result. Restoring valid input returns a previously calculated lane's estimate.

Each lane owns transient entries. Newly opened lanes are blank; switching back retains entered figures. Review, failed-run and transfer synthetic estimates all matched the existing model outputs.

Assumptions expand normally; scenario clearing invalidates the range and estimate. Reset restores illustrative 10–25 and result. Expanded details may require scrolling. Contact capture stays in the comprehensive stage; contact forms were not filled or submitted during this revision.
