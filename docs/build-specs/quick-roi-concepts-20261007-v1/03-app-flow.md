## Shared phase-one flow
1. Arrive directly at the tool on the new preview URL. One sentence explains the job and states that results are available without contact details.
2. Enter three to five operating inputs, or inspect a clearly labelled example. Defaults distinguish user values from assumptions.
3. Select 'See my estimate' or receive immediate recalculation, according to the chosen concept.
4. See the recovered-effort range, potential capacity value, dominant driver and a short visible explanation of the planning assumption.
5. Open assumptions or edit inputs without losing the result. Optionally provide an investment amount for an investment comparison; cash ROI requires a cash-realization basis.
6. Choose 'Explore full ROI'. The next stage explains that it covers investment scope, failure attribution, review, transfer and realized savings. An optional contact path appears only here, with clear purpose and no required marketing opt-in to keep the initial estimate.
7. Continue into the existing deeper assessment or keep the quick result. No automatic email, external submission or sales notification occurs in the concept stage.

## Concept variations
A starts with a compact batch-effort form and one adjacent result region.
B starts with a pain choice, then reveals one lane's three questions. Switching lanes retains a clearly scoped result; estimates from separate lanes are not summed.
C starts with weekly effort and team scope. An exposed 48-week planning year prevents confusing hours/person with hours/team.

## States and recovery
- Empty: clear labels, examples and no fabricated customer result.
- Example: sample values and results explicitly labelled illustrative.
- Invalid: explain the affected field and preserve other entries. Zero failures and zero avoidable hours are allowed; no artificial minimum to force positive value.
- Low/zero value: honest zero or low opportunity result, with useful next steps and no pressure CTA.
- Assumptions: visible range, editable inputs and reset control.
- Next-stage declined: initial estimate remains accessible.
- Optional lead failure: keep the estimate and entered form values, offer retry, and accurately state whether anything was saved.

## Completion criteria
Phase one completes when the visitor can explain the result and its main assumption. Entering an email is not required for completion. Phase two completes only after the deeper assessment's own validation and report flow; a click into that flow does not prove completion.
