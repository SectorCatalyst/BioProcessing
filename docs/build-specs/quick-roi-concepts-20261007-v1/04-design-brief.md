## Audience and emotional brief
Audience: busy bioprocess leaders, scientists, engineers and operations managers evaluating whether a discussion is worthwhile. Starting concern is assumed to be effort, data availability and distrust of optimistic ROI claims. Intended response is curiosity followed by control and confidence. Governing sentence: make a small, recognizable operating input produce an understandable value range that the visitor can inspect and challenge.

## Existing direction and constraints
Use the project-specific Yokogawa logo as an unchanged source asset, with clearspace, on white. Preserve the existing legible sans-serif direction (Plus Jakarta Sans / Space Grotesk intent) and restrained #004F9B blue, dark navy and neutral surfaces. Yellow is a small accent, never the only signal. The existing screenshot is a brand reference, not a layout to copy: remove contact-first gating, oversized introductory copy and excessive panel framing.

## Three treatments
Batch Value Snapshot: a plain white tool surface, compact heading, left-aligned form and one result region. Use strong input-to-output mapping and generous whitespace. No centered outer app card, nested cards or decorative scientific imagery. Recommended first direction.
Bottleneck Finder: a compact three-way pain choice above the contextual form, with one lane's result. Prioritize relevance and recognition; avoid a full maturity survey.
Team Time Back: a calm guided weekly-effort interaction with one active question or compact three-field row, and a larger time-recovered result. The transition to the deeper assessment is an invitation, not a gate.

## Journey and cue map
| Moment | Concern | Treatment | Accessible equivalent | Validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Will this take too long? | Show small field count, clear job and 'No contact details needed' | Plain text and labelled controls | Observe first action and expectations |

| Result | Is this inflated? | Range, scope, capacity label and one visible scenario assumption | Textual result, no color-only meaning | Ask users to explain the estimate |

| Next stage | Am I committing to sales? | Explain full-assessment value before optional contact | Clear link/button labels and consent purpose | Observe voluntary transition and understanding |

## Concept rendering specification
Generate three independent desktop UI mockups at a requested 1440 × 1024 composition. Attach the current public-entry screenshot and approved local logo as actual references. Each shows one focused primary screen, illustrative numerical inputs, one primary action and at most two supporting actions. No five-year IRR/NPV, oversized ROI percentage, invented social proof, real customer details or unapproved product price. Generated text/logo accuracy is checked before selection; exact source assets replace raster imitations in any later implementation.

## Validation plan and guidance used
Premium UI Stack coordinates UI Doctrine, UX Laws, emotional design, Product Design get-context/ideate, Yokogawa branding and image-generation guidance. The six-document harness applies before implementation. Mockups are evaluated for hierarchy, readable units, simple entry, trustworthy scope labels and a clear deeper-assessment invitation. After selection, implement and test desktop/mobile, keyboard, focus, reduced motion if applicable, validation and all important states. No usability or conversion gain is claimed from image generation.
